# Permit-to-Work System

**Module:** Safety / HSE  
**Owner:** HSE Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2025-04-18  
**Status:** Current

## 6 PTW Types
| Colour | Type | Validity | Authoriser |
|--------|------|----------|------------|
| Red | Energy Isolation / LOTO | 12 hours | HSE Officer + Electrical Super. |
| Yellow | Hot Work | 8 hours | HSE Officer |
| Blue | Confined Space | 8 hours | HSE Officer + Rescue Standby |
| Green | Ground Disturbance | 24 hours | Mine Super. |
| White | Working at Height | 12 hours | HSE Officer |
| Orange | Chemical Handling | 24 hours | HSE Officer |

## LOTO Procedure
1. Identify all isolation points from LOTO diagram
2. De-energise in correct sequence
3. Each worker applies their own personal lock
4. Tag with name, date, time, work order
5. Test isolation — attempt to start
6. Release stored energy

## Confined Space Entry
- Gas test before entry and continuously
- Standby person with rescue equipment
- Communication check every 5 minutes
- Emergency retrieval system rigged

## Zero-Tolerance Violations
- Removing another person's lock
- Signing without site visit
- Working outside permit scope
- Expired permit still active

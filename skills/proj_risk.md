# Risk Register & Lessons Learned

**Module:** Projects  
**Owner:** General Manager / PMO  
**Last Updated:** 2025-02-28  
**Status:** Current

## Risk Register Format
- Risk ID, description, category (technical, schedule, cost, HSE)
- Probability (1-5), Impact (1-5), Risk Score (P × I)
- Response strategy: avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept
- Risk owner, review date, action plan

## Top 10 Mining Project Risks
1. Geotechnical uncertainty
2. Equipment delivery delays
3. Regulatory approval delays
4. Cost escalation (fuel, steel, explosives)
5. Workforce availability
6. Water management
7. Community relations
8. Commodity price volatility
9. Contractor performance
10. Technology integration

## EMV (Expected Monetary Value) Calculation
- Risk value = Probability × Impact (RM)
- Contingency = Sum of all risk EMVs
- Management reserve = 5% of base estimate

## Lessons Learned Process
- Captured at each gate review
- Categories: what went well, what went wrong, what to improve
- Lessons stored in searchable database
- Mandatory review before similar new projects

# New Hire Onboarding Protocol

**Module:** HRM  
**Owner:** HR Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2025-04-05  
**Status:** Current

## Day 1 Orientation
- Welcome pack: offer letter, handbook, ID card, PPE
- Site safety induction (mandatory before any site access)
- IT setup: email, KROS access, CMMS, HRMS
- Buddy assigned for first 2 weeks

## 30/60/90 Check-ins
| Period | Focus | Reviewer |
|--------|-------|----------|
| 30 days | Role clarity, initial feedback | HR + Supervisor |
| 60 days | Competency check, training needs | Supervisor |
| 90 days | Confirmation decision | Department Head + HR |

## Buddy System
- Volunteer from same department
- Duration: minimum 2 weeks
- Checklist: facility tour, key contacts, system walkthroughs
- Buddy receives recognition in performance review

## Knowledge Capture
- New hires identify knowledge gaps in SKILL.md files
- Gaps logged and assigned to skill owners
- Updates reviewed at quarterly KROS review

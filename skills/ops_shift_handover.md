# Shift Handover Protocol

**Module:** Operations  
**Owner:** Ops Superintendent  
**Last Updated:** 2025-04-28  
**Status:** Current

## 6-Section Handover Form (OPS-S01)

### Section A — Production Status
- Actual vs plan: tonnes mined, processed, metres drilled
- Carry-forward tasks and reasons
- Current mining faces / active areas

### Section B — Equipment Status
- Available equipment for next shift
- Equipment under maintenance with ETA
- Defect reports raised

### Section C — Safety & Compliance
- All active PTWs listed with expiry times
- Active isolations / locked-out equipment
- Near-misses or incidents

### Section D — People
- Headcount on site
- Staff welfare concerns
- Contractor activities

### Section E — Outstanding Actions
- Previous shift actions status
- Urgent actions for incoming shift

### Section F — Sign-off
- Outgoing supervisor signature + time
- Incoming supervisor signature + time

**Zero-Gap Rule:** Minimum 5-minute verbal briefing required. Form does not replace face-to-face handover.

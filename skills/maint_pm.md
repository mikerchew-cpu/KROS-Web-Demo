# Preventive Maintenance Schedule

**Module:** Maintenance  
**Owner:** Maintenance Super.  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Stale

## PM Intervals by Equipment Type
| Equipment | Daily | Weekly | Monthly | Quarterly |
|-----------|-------|--------|---------|-----------|
| Crusher | Visual, temp | Lube levels | Oil sample | Liner check |
| Conveyor | Belt tracking | Idler check | Tension | Structure |
| Excavator | Grease, fluid | Track tension | Swing bearing | Major service |
| Haul Truck | Tyre, fluid | Brake test | Engine oil | Transmission |

## Oil Analysis Programme
- Sampling frequency: every 250 operating hours
- Tests: viscosity, contamination, wear metals
- Red flags: water > 0.1%, iron > 100 ppm

## Maintenance KPIs
- PM compliance target: > 95%
- Breakdown rate: < 2 per month per major equipment
- Mean Time Between Failures: tracked monthly

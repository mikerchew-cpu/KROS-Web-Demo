## Malaysian State Royalty Rates
| Mineral | Rate | Basis |
|---------|------|-------|
| Gold | 5% | Gross sales value |
| Iron ore | 3% | Gross sales value |
| Aggregate | RM 2.00/tonne | Production volume |
| Tin | 5% | Gross sales value |

## Payment Schedule
- State royalty: monthly, 15th of following month
- EPF/SOCSO/HRDF/PCB: 15th of following month
- Annual tax: monthly instalments (CP 204)

## EPF Detailed Calculation
| Age | Employer | Employee |
|-----|----------|----------|
| < 60 | 13% of monthly wages | 11% (or 0% if opted) |
| ≥ 60 | 6.5% | 5.5% (or 11%/13% if opted) |

## PCB (Income Tax Deduction)
- Monthly deduction based on PCB schedule
- Annual return via e-BE/e-C by 30 April
- Employer submits CP 39 monthly

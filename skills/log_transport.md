# Transport & Logistics

**Module:** logistics  
**Owner:** Logistics Manager  
**Status:** Current  
**Sensitivity:** low

Procedures for product dispatch, haulage management, and logistics coordination.

---

## Product Dispatch

### Loading Procedures
1. Verify product specification matches customer order
2. Inspect vehicle condition and cleanliness before loading
3. Control loading rate to prevent spillage and overloading
4. Record loaded weight via certified weighbridge
5. Issue delivery note and certificate of analysis

### Weighbridge Operations
| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| Gross weight | Weigh vehicle fully loaded |
| Tare weight | Weigh empty vehicle after unloading (or use registered tare) |
| Net weight | Gross - Tare = Product weight |
| Ticket | Issue weighbridge ticket with all weights, date, time, product |

## Haulage Management

### Haul Road Standards
- Maintained width: minimum 3.5x widest vehicle
- Gradient: maximum 8% for loaded haul trucks
- Surface: compacted, watered for dust control
- Signs: speed limits, junction warnings, hazard markers

### Vehicle Tracking
- GPS tracking on all haul vehicles
- Real-time location monitoring from dispatch office
- Automated alerts for route deviations
- Cycle time tracking for performance analysis

## Contractor Logistics

### Contractor Vehicle Requirements
- Valid road tax and insurance
- Vehicle inspection certificate (annual)
- Driver licence verified for vehicle class
- Site induction completed
- Emergency contact details on file

### Loading & Scheduling
- Appointment system for all third-party collections
- Priority allocation: export > domestic > stockpile
- Demurrage policy applies after 2 hours waiting time
- Loading rate target: <30 minutes per vehicle

## Export Logistics

### Documentation Requirements
| Document | Responsible Party |
|----------|------------------|
| Commercial invoice | Finance |
| Packing list | Logistics |
| Bill of lading | Shipping agent |
| Certificate of origin | Chamber of Commerce |
| Certificate of analysis | Quality / Laboratory |

### Port Coordination
- Book vessel slot minimum 14 days in advance
- Arrange truck/rail scheduling to meet vessel loading plan
- Coordinate with shipping agent for customs clearance
- Ensure all export permits are valid

## Reporting & KPIs

| KPI | Target |
|-----|--------|
| On-time dispatch | >95% |
| Loading time | <30 min per vehicle |
| Demurrage incidents | <5 per month |
| Haulage cost per tonne | Within budget |
| Customer delivery claim | <1% of shipments |

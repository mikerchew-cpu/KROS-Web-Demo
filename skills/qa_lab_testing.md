# Laboratory Testing Procedures

**Module:** quality  
**Owner:** Laboratory Manager  
**Status:** Current  
**Sensitivity:** low

Standard methods for on-site laboratory testing of ore, concentrate, and aggregate samples.

---

## Sample Reception & Preparation

### Receiving Protocol
1. Verify sample label matches submission form
2. Log sample into laboratory information system
3. Assign unique sample ID
4. Record sample weight, appearance, and any anomalies

### Preparation Steps
| Step | Process | Equipment |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Drying | 105°C until constant weight | Drying oven |
| Crushing | Reduce to <2mm | Jaw crusher / Ring mill |
| Splitting | Obtain representative sub-sample | Riffle splitter / Rotary divider |
| Pulverising | Reduce to <75µm | Ring/puck mill |

## Analytical Methods

### Moisture Content
- Oven drying method at 105°C ± 5°C
- Calculate: (Wet weight - Dry weight) / Wet weight × 100

### Grade Analysis
| Element | Method | Standard |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Iron (Fe) | XRF | ISO 9516 |
| Silica (SiO₂) | XRF | ISO 9516 |
| Alumina (Al₂O₃) | XRF | ISO 9516 |
| Loss on Ignition | Gravimetric | 1000°C for 1 hour |

### Physical Testing
| Test | Method | Equipment |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Particle size distribution | Sieve analysis | Sieve shaker |
| Specific gravity | Pycnometer | Le Chatelier flask |
| Bulk density | Measured cylinder | Weigh scale |

## Quality Control

### Internal QC
- Duplicate analysis every 10th sample (acceptance: ±5% RSD)
- Certified reference material every 20 samples
- Blank analysis every batch
- Re-analysis when QC limits are exceeded

### External QC
- Inter-laboratory comparison quarterly
- Proficiency testing programme annually
- External audit every 2 years

## Reporting

| Report | Contents | Recipient |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Routine Analysis | Grade, moisture, size distribution | Plant Manager, Quality Manager |
| Shipment Certificate | Full specification with QC data | Customer |
| Monthly Summary | All results with statistics | Quality Manager, Mine Manager |

## Safety

- Wear appropriate PPE at all times
- Use fume hood for acid digestion procedures
- Dispose of chemical waste per environmental procedures
- Maintain Material Safety Data Sheets for all chemicals

## Data Sources

- Laboratory Information Management System
- Certified reference material certificates
- Instrument calibration records
- QC charts and control limits

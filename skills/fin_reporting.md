# Management Accounts & Reporting

**Module:** Finance  
**Owner:** Finance Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2025-04-12  
**Status:** Current

## Monthly Management Accounts Structure
1. Executive Summary — key variances, YTD performance
2. Income Statement — revenue, cost of sales, gross profit, operating costs
3. Balance Sheet — cash, receivables, payables, provisions
4. Cash Flow Statement — operating, investing, financing
5. Production Report — tonnes mined, processed, sold
6. Unit Cost Analysis — RM/tonne by activity
7. Capital Expenditure — budget vs actual, committed

## Key Metrics
- Gross margin target: > 25%
- Operating cost per tonne: tracked monthly
- Working capital days: < 45 days
- EBITDA margin: tracked against budget

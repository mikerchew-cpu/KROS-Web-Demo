## Complaint Classification

### Severity Levels
| Level | Definition | Response Time | Escalation |
|-------|------------|---------------|------------|
| L1 | Minor issue, no immediate impact | 7 days | Community Relations Officer |
| L2 | Moderate concern, potential escalation | 3 days | Community Relations Manager |
| L3 | Significant issue, media/public risk | 24 hours | Mine Manager |
| L4 | Critical emergency, safety or legal | Immediate | Senior Management + Legal |

### Complaint Categories
- Environmental: dust, noise, vibration, water quality
- Social: employment, contractor behaviour, traffic
- Infrastructure: road damage, drainage, property damage
- Cultural: heritage site disturbance, burial ground
- Economic: local business impact, compensation
- Other: any concern not covered above

## Investigation Procedure

### Steps
1. Acknowledge receipt and assign complaint number
2. Assign investigator based on complaint category
3. Site visit within required response time
4. Interview complainant and gather evidence
5. Document findings in complaint management system
6. Determine corrective actions with relevant department
7. Communicate findings and proposed resolution to complainant
8. Implement corrective actions
9. Verify resolution effectiveness within 30 days

### Documentation
Each complaint record must include:
- Complaint number, date, and channel
- Complainant details (name, contact, village)
- Category and severity classification
- Description of issue
- Investigation findings with photos/maps
- Corrective action plan with timeline
- Closure confirmation with complainant signature

## Escalation Process

### Internal Escalation
```
Complainant → Community Relations Officer → Community Relations Manager
→ Mine Manager → Senior Management → Board of Directors
```

### External Escalation
If complainant is not satisfied after resolution:
- Offer mediation with third-party facilitator
- Refer to District Office or relevant government agency
- Legal process as last resort

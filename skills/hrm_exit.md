# Exit Knowledge Capture Protocol

**Module:** HRM  
**Owner:** HR Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2025-04-20  
**Status:** Current

## 5-Step Exit Process
1. **Notice Received** → HR schedules exit interview + generates skill handover plan
2. **Knowledge Audit** → Identify which SKILL.md files the departing person owns or contributes to
3. **AI-Assisted Exit Interview** → Structured interview with AI-generated probes based on their skills
4. **Knowledge Transfer** → Departing person updates assigned SKILL.md files + briefs successor
5. **Clearance & Handover** → Systems access revoked, SKILL.md ownership transferred, final check completed

## Interview Questions (AI-Generated)
Based on role and skills, the AI generates:
- Process-specific questions ("How do you decide when to override the feed rate?")
- Tacit knowledge probes ("What warning signs would you look for that are not in the SOP?")
- Relationship mapping ("Which external contacts are critical for this role?")

## SKILL.md Update Assignments
- Departing person must update a minimum of 2 SKILL.md files
- New owner assigned in KROS before final clearance
- HR verifies updates are completed

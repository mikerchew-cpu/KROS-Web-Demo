# Environmental Reporting (DOE/JMG)

**Module:** Environment  
**Owner:** Environmental Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2025-03-30  
**Status:** Current

## DOE Reporting Calendar
| Report | Frequency | Deadline |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Effluent discharge monitoring | Monthly | 7th of next month |
| Scheduled waste inventory | Quarterly | 15th after quarter end |
| Environmental Compliance Report | Annual | 31 March |
| EIA monitoring | Per condition | As per DOE approval |

## Effluent Standards (Standard B)
- pH: 5.5 - 9.0
- TSS: < 100 mg/L
- Oil & grease: < 10 mg/L
- COD: < 200 mg/L
- Heavy metals per schedule

## TSF Management
- Freeboard: minimum 1m maintained
- Inspection: daily visual, weekly structured
- Piezometer readings weekly
- Dam safety review: annual by certified engineer

## Scheduled Waste
- Inventory kept on-site (Form JAS 3)
- Storage: labelled, bunded, covered
- Disposal: DOE-licensed contractor only
- e-SWIS submission: within 30 days of generation

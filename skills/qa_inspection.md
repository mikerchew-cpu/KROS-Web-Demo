# Quality Inspection Procedures

**Module:** quality  
**Owner:** Quality Manager  
**Status:** Current  
**Sensitivity:** low

Inspection procedures for product quality across the mining and processing value chain.

---

## Inspection Points

| Stage | Inspection Type | Frequency | Responsible |
|-------|----------------|-----------|-------------|
| ROM Pad | Visual grade assessment | Per truck | Loader Operator |
| Crusher Feed | Oversize detection | Continuous | Crusher Operator |
| Conveyor | Sampling for lab | Every 2 hours | Plant Technician |
| Stockpile | Grade blending verification | Per shift | Shift Supervisor |
| Load-out | Customer specification check | Per load | Weighbridge Officer |

## Sampling Protocol

### Sample Collection
1. Collect minimum 5 kg sample from designated sampling point
2. Use riffle splitter to obtain 1 kg laboratory sample
3. Label with date, time, location, and sampler name
4. Submit to laboratory within 2 hours of collection

### Sample Frequency
| Product | Minimum Frequency | Method |
|---------|-----------------|--------|
| Crushed ore | Every 500 tonnes | Belt cut |
| Concentrate | Every batch | Stream sampling |
| Aggregate | Daily composite | Stockpile grid |

## Acceptance Criteria

### Ore Quality
| Parameter | Target | Acceptable Range | Reject Limit |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| Grade (Fe) | 58% | 56-60% | <54% |
| Moisture | 8% | 6-10% | >12% |
| Oversize (>150mm) | 0% | <3% | >5% |

### Aggregate Quality
| Test | Standard | Acceptance |
|------|----------|------------|
| Los Angeles Abrasion | <40% | Pass |
| Flakiness Index | <25% | Pass |
| Water Absorption | <2% | Pass |

## Non-Conformance Handling

When product fails to meet specifications:
1. Isolate non-conforming material
2. Tag with hold label and record in quality log
3. Notify Shift Supervisor and Quality Manager
4. Determine disposition: re-process, blend, or reject
5. Implement corrective action to prevent recurrence

## Documentation

- Daily quality reports filed by shift
- Certificate of Analysis for each customer shipment
- Non-conformance reports with root cause and corrective action
- Monthly quality performance summary

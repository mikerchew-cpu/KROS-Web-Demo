## Stock Management

### Reorder Levels
Each item has defined:
- **Minimum stock** — level triggering reorder
- **Reorder quantity** — optimal order size (EOQ)
- **Maximum stock** — storage capacity or working capital limit
- **Lead time** — supplier delivery time for reorder planning

### Stock Classification (ABC Analysis)
| Class | % of Items | % of Value | Management Focus |
|-------|-----------|------------|------------------|
| A | 10% | 70% | Tight control, frequent review |
| B | 20% | 20% | Moderate control, monthly review |
| C | 70% | 10% | Simplified control, quarterly review |

## Receiving & Issuing

### Goods Receiving
1. Verify delivery against PO — item, quantity, condition
2. Inspect for damage or defects
3. Record batch/serial numbers for traceable items
4. Update inventory system within 2 hours
5. Place in designated storage location

### Issuing Procedure
1. Requisition form must be approved by authorised person
2. Record item, quantity, cost centre, and requestor
3. Update inventory system immediately
4. Apply FIFO principle for perishable items
5. Sign out high-value or controlled items individually

## Stocktake

| Type | Frequency | Scope |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Cycle count | Daily | A-class items |
| Partial count | Weekly | A + B class items |
| Full stocktake | Monthly | All items |
| Annual audit | Yearly | 100% verification with external auditor |

# AI HR Analytics

**Module:** hrm  
**Owner:** HR Manager  
**Status:** Current  
**Sensitivity:** high

AI-driven workforce analytics, attrition prediction, competency gap detection, and training recommendations.

---

## Attrition Risk Prediction

The AI engine analyses employee data to predict departure risk and identify retention actions:

- **Flight risk scoring** — composite score based on tenure, engagement, performance, and external factors
- **Pattern recognition** — identifies common characteristics of past departures
- **Early warning system** — flags employees showing pre-departure behaviour patterns
- **Department-level risk** — aggregate attrition probability by team and role

### Attrition Risk Levels
| Risk Level | Score | Response |
|------------|-------|----------|
| 🔴 Critical | >75% | Immediate retention interview + manager escalation |
| 🟡 Warning | 50-75% | Schedule career development conversation |
| 🟢 Monitor | <50% | Standard engagement activities |

## Competency Gap Detection

AI continuously maps workforce competencies against role requirements:

- **Gap analysis** — automated comparison of assessed competencies vs required competencies per role
- **Succession readiness** — identifies gaps preventing deputies from being "Ready Now"
- **Critical skill coverage** — monitors number of trained personnel per zero-tolerance competency
- **Expiry tracking** — alerts when certifications and competency assessments are approaching expiry

### Competency Heat Map
The AI generates a department-level heat map showing:
- Roles with the largest competency gaps (red)
- Competencies with fewest qualified personnel (orange)
- Upcoming expiry clusters (yellow)
- Fully covered areas (green)

## Training Needs Analysis

### Automated Recommendations
- Recommend specific training programmes based on identified gaps
- Suggest training priority order based on risk impact of unfilled gaps
- Identify cross-training opportunities between departments
- Propose optimal training schedule to minimise operational disruption

### Effectiveness Tracking
- Correlate training completion with incident rate reduction
- Track post-training competency assessment scores
- Measure time-to-competency for new hires vs training path
- Recommend refresher frequency based on knowledge decay analysis

## Workforce Planning

### Demand Forecasting
- Predict hiring needs based on attrition projections and growth plans
- Identify critical roles with <2 qualified successors
- Recommend recruitment timelines based on training lead times

### Optimisation Suggestions
- Shift allocation recommendations based on workload patterns
- Overtime anomaly detection and cost projections
- Cross-department resource sharing opportunities

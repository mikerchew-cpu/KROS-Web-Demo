## HAZOP Guide Words
- NO / NOT, MORE, LESS, AS WELL AS, PART OF, REVERSE, OTHER THAN

## Site Risk Register Format
- Risk ID, Description, Category, Current Controls
- L (1-5), C (1-5), RPN (L × C)
- Risk Owner, Review Date, Action Plan

## Bowtie Analysis
- Left side: threats and preventive controls
- Top event: loss of control
- Right side: consequences and mitigative controls

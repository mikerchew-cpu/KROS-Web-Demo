# Payroll, Leave & Allowances

**Module:** HRM  
**Owner:** HR Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2025-04-01  
**Status:** Current

## Payroll Calendar
- Pay date: 25th of each month
- Cut-off: 20th for timesheet submission
- OT claims due by 22nd
- HR review (24th), Finance reviewed (24th), approved (25th)

## OT Calculation
- Weekday: 1.5 × hourly for first 4 hrs, 2.0 × thereafter
- Weekend: 2.0 × hourly
- Public holiday: 3.0 × hourly

## Malaysian Statutory Compliance
| Contribution | Employer | Employee | Cap |
|-------------|----------|----------|-----|
| EPF (< 60) | 13% | 11% | RM 30,000/month |
| EPF (≥ 60) | 6.5% | 5.5% | RM 30,000/month |
| SOCSO | 1.75% | 0.5% | RM 5,000/month |
| EIS | 0.2% | 0.2% | RM 5,000/month |
| HRDF | 1% | — | All registered employers |

## HRDF Levy
- Registered employers: 1% of monthly wages
- Submission: monthly via HRDF portal
- Deadline: 15th of following month
- Levies can be used for training grants

# Community Relations

**Module:** community  
**Owner:** Community Relations Manager  
**Status:** Current  
**Sensitivity:** medium

Procedures for managing community engagement, stakeholder communication, and corporate social responsibility.

---

## Stakeholder Identification

### Key Stakeholder Groups
| Group | Interest | Engagement Frequency |
|-------|----------|---------------------|
| Local communities | Employment, environment, compensation | Monthly |
| Traditional landowners | Land rights, cultural heritage | Quarterly |
| Local government | Licences, taxes, development | Monthly |
| State government | Royalties, permits, compliance | Quarterly |
| NGOs / Civil society | Environment, human rights | As needed |
| Employees / Unions | Working conditions, wages | Ongoing |

## Consultation Procedures

### Meeting Protocols
1. Publish meeting notice minimum 7 days in advance
2. Hold meetings in local language
3. Provide written summary of discussions
4. Document concerns and commitments in minutes
5. Follow up on action items within agreed timeframe

### Consultation Types
| Type | Purpose | Typical Attendees |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| Village forum | General updates, Q&A | Community members (open) |
| Focus group | Specific issue discussion | Affected households |
| Liaison committee | Ongoing relationship management | Elected representatives |
| Open day | Site tour, information sharing | Public |

## Grievance Management

### Grievance Categories
- Environmental impact concerns
- Employment and contractor issues
- Land and compensation disputes
- Infrastructure damage complaints
- Cultural heritage concerns

### Resolution Process
1. Register grievance within 24 hours
2. Acknowledge receipt to complainant within 48 hours
3. Investigate and propose resolution within 14 days
4. Communicate outcome and implement agreed actions
5. Close grievance with complainant sign-off
6. Record lessons learned and share with team

| Education | School supplies, scholarships, library | RM 50,000 |
